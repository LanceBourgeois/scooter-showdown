🛴 BC E-Scooter Rules
Before the first ride 🛴

The scooter rules in BC

The actual law for riding an electric kick scooter in British Columbia — plain and simple, for Phoenix & Dad to go through together. Two big ones first, because they're the two you'll most want to bend.

⚠️ The 2 rules to know first
You must be
16+
You have to be 16 or older to legally ride on public roads & paths. You're 13 — so for now that means private property only (driveways, yards, off-road spots), not the street.
Max speed
25
25 km/h is the legal limit on public roads & paths — even though your scooter goes way faster. That extra speed is for private property, not out in public.
🔎 Straight talk: these are exactly the two things that are tempting to ignore — being under 16, and opening it up past 25. Breaking either can mean a ticket, a fine, or getting hurt. Know them, own them.

⛑️ Helmet = mandatory, every ride

An approved safety helmet is required by law — no exceptions, every single ride. (A full-face / chin-bar helmet is even better at speed.)

🏎️ About your KUKIRIN specifically

BC's rules are written for scooters with a ≤500W motor and a ≤25 km/h top speed. Your KUKIRIN is more powerful and faster than that — so on public roads it doesn't legally count as an "electric kick scooter."

What that means in real life: it's a private-property machine — driveways, yards, private lots, off-road trails where allowed. That's actually where you can safely use its speed anyway. Save the fast stuff for there.

The details

Where you can & can't ride

Once you're 16 and riding a legal scooter, here's the map.

✅ You CAN ride

  • 🚲Bike lanes — always the first choice
  • 🛣️Roads posted 50 km/h or under — in the bike lane, or as far right as is safe
  • 🅿️Private property — anytime (this is your lane for now)

🚫 You CANNOT ride

  • 🚶Sidewalks (unless a sign says OK)
  • 🚻Crosswalks (unless a sign says OK)
  • 💨Roads over 50 km/h — unless there's a bike lane
The rest of the rulebook

The other rules

👤One rider only. No passengers, and don't tow people or anything else.
💡Lights on, sunset to sunrise. Front and rear lights are required after dark.
🔔Bell or horn required. You have to be able to warn people you're passing.
🖐️Signal your turns. Hand signals or built-in turn lights.
↩️Left turns = "hook" turns. Go straight through, stop, then turn 90° and cross — don't cut across traffic.
📵No phone while riding. Distracted riding is illegal, same as driving.
🚭Never impaired. No riding under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
↔️Single file. Don't ride beside another rider except to pass.
If you break the rules

The penalties are real

$109
standard violation ticket
up to $2,000
+ your scooter can be impounded

And the bigger cost isn't the fine — it's ending up hurt, or hurting someone else. Not worth it.

📍 Good news — Victoria & Saanich are in

Both Victoria and Saanich are part of BC's electric kick scooter pilot (running since April 2024), along with Oak Bay, Colwood, Langford, Esquimalt, View Royal, Sidney and more. So once you're 16 with a legal scooter, riding here is allowed — as long as you follow the rules above.
