So… which one?

🛡️
My pick for you
NAVEE ST3
Smarter and safer to own. It has a phone app, an Apple Find My tracker, and a Bluetooth lock — so if it ever walks off, you can find it. Better-reviewed tech, higher water resistance, turn signals, and a real 2025 support system. A little slower, but the more refined ride.
🔥
Strong alternative
KUKIRIN G2
If pure performance is what excites you: it's faster (45 km/h), has a bigger battery, a stronger rated motor, climbs steeper hills, front disc brake, and a loud electric horn. The trade-off: no app, no anti-theft tracking, and thinner reviews.

Both are genuinely good. The ST3 wins on brains & safety; the G2 wins on raw muscle. There's no wrong answer here — it's about what you value more.

📉 Real-world range ≠ the advertised number

Both makers measure range at unrealistically slow, steady speeds. Independent testers consistently get roughly half the claim when you actually ride normally.

For the NAVEE family, testers (ERideHero) measured ~40 km real vs the Pro's ~75 km claim. The KUKIRIN's ~55 km claim shrinks the same way in owner reports (~30-ish km real). Plan for about 50–60% of the sticker number — and less if you ride fast or uphill a lot.

You asked specifically

🔊 Horn vs. bell — and can you upgrade?

One has a loud button-horn; the other just has a bell. Here's the fix.

KUKIRIN G2 → Electric horn ✅

Loud, button-activated horn on the handlebar. Better for busy roads out of the box.

NAVEE ST3 → Mechanical bell 🔔

Comes with a simple ring-bell lever on the left grip — quieter than a horn.

Good news: you can add a clamp-on horn. Rechargeable/battery handlebar horns that fit standard 22–32 mm bars are sold on Amazon and just bolt on — no wiring needed. So the ST3's quiet bell isn't a dealbreaker.

Heads-up: the ST3's left grip is already busy (bell + turn-signal buttons), so mount the horn on a clear part of the bar or the stem. Double-check your bar's clamp size before buying.

🔐 Which one can you lock & track from your phone?

Only the NAVEE ST3. It pairs to the NAVEE app with a Bluetooth e-lock and Apple Find My phone tracking (iPhone). Walk up and it unlocks; if it's ever stolen, you can locate it.

The KUKIRIN G2 has no official app, no app-lock, and no tracking. (There's an unofficial third-party Android app floating around, but it's not from KUKIRIN and adds no lock or tracking.)

Real talk, because it matters

🦺 Ride-smart note

Both scooters are rated for ages 16+ and both go faster than 40 km/h — that's genuinely fast. A few non-negotiables:

  • ⛑️Always wear a helmet. A full-face/chin-bar helmet is smart at these speeds.
  • 🐢Start in the lowest speed mode until it feels like second nature, then step up.
  • 📍Check the local rules. In BC/Victoria, e-scooters on public roads are generally limited to ~24 km/h — so top speed is for legal, safe spots, not the street.
  • 👀Ride predictably, use the lights & signals, and assume cars can't see you.
