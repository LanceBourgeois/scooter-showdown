🚀Off the line, the Pro is a rocket. It hits 0–32 km/h in 6.8 seconds, and its peak power is actually HIGHER — 1350W vs the G2's ~1200W. "Rated" is just the cruising number; peak is the kick you feel when you gun it. The Pro's kick is bigger.
🎯It puts the power down without spinning out. The Pro has app-controlled traction control — gun it from a stop, or ride on wet or loose ground, and the wheel grips instead of spinning. The G2 has none. That's usable power, not just watts on a spec sheet.

🛡️What really kills the fun isn't top speed — it's downtime. Getting it stolen, flat tires, a dead controller, waiting weeks for parts. The Pro has Find My + a phone lock (so it doesn't get stolen), a fire-safe UL2272 battery, self-healing tires, and real 9/10 reviews behind it. More days actually riding > 5 extra km/h you'll rarely touch.

Bottom line: you didn't get the slower scooter. You got the one that's just as quick where it counts, hits harder off the line, rides smoother, and won't leave you stranded — or stolen. That's the better fast scooter. 🤘

📉 Real-world range ≠ the advertised number

Every brand measures range at unrealistically slow, steady speeds. Independent testers get roughly half the claim when you ride normally.

Your ST3 Pro is advertised at 75 km — but ERideHero measured ~40 km real (24.9 miles) at a normal pace. Plan for about 40 km per charge, less if you ride fast or uphill a lot. Totally normal — every scooter in this class does the same.

🔊 Horn / bell — and how to go louder

The G2 has a loud button-horn; the NAVEE uses a bell/beep. Easy to upgrade.

KUKIRIN G2 → Electric horn ✅

Loud, button-activated horn on the handlebar — great for busy roads out of the box.

NAVEE ST3 Pro → Built-in bell/beep 🔔

Comes with a built-in bell/beep on the handlebar — handy, but quieter than a car-style horn.

Want louder? You can add a clamp-on horn. Rechargeable handlebar horns that fit standard 22–32 mm bars are on Amazon and just bolt on — no wiring needed.

The left grip is a bit busy (bell + turn-signal buttons), so mount the horn on a clear part of the bar or the stem. Check your bar's clamp size before buying.

🔐 Your ST3 Pro can be locked & tracked from your phone

The NAVEE ST3 Pro pairs to the NAVEE app with a Bluetooth e-lock and Apple Find My tracking (iPhone). Walk up and it unlocks; if it's ever stolen, you can locate it. Worth setting up on day one.

The KUKIRIN G2 has none of that — no app, no lock, no tracking. One of the biggest reasons the Pro is the better buy.

Real talk, because it matters

🦺 Ride-smart note

Your ST3 Pro does 40 km/h — genuinely fast. It's rated for ages 16+, so ease in and ride it smart:

  • ⛑️Always wear a helmet. A full-face / chin-bar helmet is smart at these speeds.
  • 🐢Start in the lowest speed mode until it feels like second nature, then step up.
  • 📍Check the local rules. In BC/Victoria, e-scooters on public roads are generally capped around 24 km/h and min age 16 — so full speed is for legal, safe, off-road spots, not the street.
  • 👀Ride predictably, use the lights & signals, and assume cars can't see you.
